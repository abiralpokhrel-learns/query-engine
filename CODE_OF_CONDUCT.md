# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a small, technical collaboration space. We are committed to
making participation respectful, constructive, and free of harassment for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or opinions
about query engines.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous with the people solving them.
- Critique code, measurements, and designs, not contributors.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the effect of your words.
- Keep discussions on topic and reproducible.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone else's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption,
sexualized attention, and publishing private information without permission are
not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, or project
spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@abiralpokhrel-learns], through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as
practical. The maintainer may remove comments, edit or lock threads, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participation. Good-faith
reports will not be retaliated against.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in
public.
